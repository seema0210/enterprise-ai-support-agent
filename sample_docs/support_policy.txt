
Enterprise AI Support Platform - Sample Support Policy

Refund Policy:
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
Refund requests must include the order number and reason.
Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days.

Account Policy:
Users must verify their email address before activating an account.
Users can request a password reset through the login page.
Support staff must not ask users to share their passwords.

Technical Support Policy:
Technical support is available Monday to Friday.
Critical system incidents are escalated to the engineering team.
High-priority incidents require an initial response within 2 hours.

Cancellation Policy:
Customers can cancel their subscription from account settings.
Cancellation stops future renewals.
Existing access continues until the end of the paid billing period.
